# Contributing to AI-First Company

## Purpose and expectations

Contributions and respectful technical discussion are welcome. The project is currently maintained with limited capacity alongside other professional responsibilities. No service level, response time, review, acceptance, or merge is guaranteed.

Prefer small, bounded, independently reviewable changes. Repository participation does not itself create authority over the Architecture or any other document layer.

All participation must follow the community expectations in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Where to participate

- **GitHub Issues** — currently used for questions, usage reports, implementation experiences, reproducible defects, broken references, inconsistencies, and bounded proposals.
- **GitHub Discussions** — intended for research exchange, exploratory proposals, and substantial changes once Discussions are enabled.
- **Pull Requests** — agreed or clearly bounded changes with an identifiable problem and verification.
- **Security vulnerabilities** — follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not disclose sensitive exploit details, secrets, or private information in a public Issue, Discussion, or Pull Request.

## Before a substantial change

Begin with prior Issue alignment, or Discussion alignment once Discussions are enabled, before changing:

- Architecture meaning;
- Concepts or Relationships;
- Reference Compositions;
- `TR-*` or `XTR-*` requirements;
- repository structure;
- broad terminology;
- large diagram designs; or
- normative artifacts.

An unsolicited large Pull Request may be closed or deferred without detailed review.

## Contribution requirements

Each contribution must identify:

- the problem and intended outcome;
- the affected authority layer;
- affected files and Scope;
- whether the change is normative, derived, explanatory, visual, or repository governance;
- applicable traces and synchronization effects;
- validation performed;
- unresolved limitations; and
- the related Discussion or Issue where prior alignment is required.

Changes must follow the authority, terminology, traceability, No-Orphan, synchronization, and validation rules in [PROJECT.md](PROJECT.md).

## AI-assisted contributions

AI-assisted contributions are allowed. The Human contributor remains responsible for correctness, Provenance, licensing, confidential information, and compliance with repository governance. AI output is review input, not an authoritative source, and material AI assistance should be disclosed briefly in the Pull Request.

Do not submit secrets, private conversations, copyrighted material without permission, fabricated citations, or unverified AI-generated claims.

## Review and acceptance

Review may request narrower Scope, supporting Evidence, traceability, terminology changes, or visual verification. Maintainer approval and merge are separate from community interest or technical plausibility. Contributions may be deferred or declined because of Scope, consistency, Evidence, capacity, maintenance burden, or timing.

Accepted contributions are subject to the repository license and attribution requirements. Future trusted contributors may receive bounded triage, review, or maintainer responsibilities when explicitly granted.
